Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Closed AI ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by the Closed AI team. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Closed AI:

  • Email address — collected at sign-up for account creation and authentication.
  • Conversation history — your chat messages are stored in our database to provide conversation continuity and memory features.
  • Credit balance and transaction records — records of credit top-ups and usage for billing purposes.
  • Usage data — which AI models you use and approximate token counts, used to calculate per-message costs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide AI chat responses by routing your messages through our secure API proxy.
  • To process payments and manage your credit balance via Razorpay.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. email verification, password reset) via Supabase Auth.
  • To maintain conversation context and memory across sessions.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure (PostgreSQL database with row-level security). Messages in transit are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and storage. Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter — routes AI model requests. Messages are processed but not stored by OpenRouter beyond the immediate request. Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Workers — our API proxy layer. Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay — payment processing. We do not store card details; all payment data is handled by Razorpay. Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data and conversation history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Closed AI is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Export your conversation history.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
